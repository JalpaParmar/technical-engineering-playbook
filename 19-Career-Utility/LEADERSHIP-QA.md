# Engineering Leadership Q&A

## Architecture disagreement
Clarify constraints/evidence, compare options/trade-offs, prototype uncertain points if economical, use accountable decision owner and record consequential decisions.

## Technical debt
Translate into incident/security risk, lead time, blocked roadmap or toil; prioritize transparently against product value.

## Mentoring
Delegate outcomes with boundaries, ask questions before prescribing, explain review rationale and match stretch work to support/risk.

## Production incident
Stabilize safely, investigate with evidence, communicate facts, validate recovery and ensure systemic follow-up.

## Rewrite proposal
Ask for measurable pain, migration/test strategy, opportunity cost and incremental alternatives before accepting rewrite risk.
