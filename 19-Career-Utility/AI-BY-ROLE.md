# How to Use AI by Role

**Developer:** code understanding, bounded implementation, tests, review, debugging, docs.  
**Tech Lead:** design alternatives, review, migration plans, incident synthesis.  
**Architect:** ADR options, failure-mode analysis, system-design review, documentation.  
**TPM:** requirements, dependencies, status, risk and incident evidence synthesis.  
**Engineering Manager:** meeting/action synthesis, engineering-health themes and planning support without automating people judgments.

Human validation remains mandatory for architecture, security, personnel and production decisions.
