# Incident Management

## Priorities
1. protect users/data/business;
2. establish command/ownership;
3. stabilize/mitigate;
4. investigate in parallel;
5. communicate known facts;
6. validate recovery;
7. learn/prevent recurrence.

Roles may include incident lead, technical investigators and communications. Keep decision log/timeline for significant incidents.

Do not delay safe mitigation merely to prove root cause first.
