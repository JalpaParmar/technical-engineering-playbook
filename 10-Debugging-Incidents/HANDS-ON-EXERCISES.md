# Debugging & Incident Exercises

1. Analyze a fictional 100% DB CPU incident; separate facts from hypotheses.
2. Build a timeline from mixed logs and deployment events.
3. Diagnose a retry storm after dependency timeout.
4. Draft stakeholder updates at detection, mitigation and recovery.
5. Write a blameless RCA with measurable corrective actions.
6. Give sanitized logs to an AI assistant and critique unsupported conclusions.
