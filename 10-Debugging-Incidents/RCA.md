# Root Cause Analysis

A useful RCA explains system conditions and improvements, not whom to blame.

## Template
Impact; timeline; detection; root cause; contributing factors; why safeguards failed; mitigation; permanent fix; preventive actions; owners/dates; validation.

Avoid “human error” as terminal cause. Ask what system/process made the error possible or hard to detect.

Corrective actions should be specific and testable, not “be more careful.”
