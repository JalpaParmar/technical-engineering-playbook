# Debugging & Incident Q&A

**First incident action?** Bound impact and establish ownership while pursuing safe stabilization; exact order depends on severity/data risk.

**Mitigate before RCA?** Yes when safe and impact is active. Root-cause proof can continue after stabilization.

**Logs vs metrics vs traces?** Contextual events vs aggregate behavior vs request paths; combine them.

**Good RCA?** Evidence-based timeline/root cause/contributing factors plus specific preventive actions and validation.

**AI in incidents?** Useful for summarizing evidence, clustering errors and proposing hypotheses, but humans validate root cause and production actions.
