# Master Scenario Bank

Practice aloud using: **clarify → assess evidence/impact → options → decision/action → validation → communication**.

1. Mobile release causes crash spike.
2. API team misses critical dependency.
3. Payment request times out after possible charge.
4. DB CPU reaches 100%.
5. Queue backlog grows rapidly.
6. Old mobile app breaks after backend change.
7. Senior engineers disagree on architecture.
8. Product adds material scope mid-sprint.
9. CI is green but production deployment fails.
10. Security scanner reports a critical dependency issue.
11. AI assistant produces unsupported project risk.
12. RAG retrieves another tenant’s document.
13. Executive asks for root cause before evidence exists.
14. Team proposes a full legacy rewrite.
15. Quality team reports many medium defects before release.

For each: identify facts vs assumptions, critical risk, owner/decision, mitigation, validation signal and stakeholder message.
