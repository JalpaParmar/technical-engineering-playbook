Requirement → architecture choice → security → reliability → observability → cost → deployment/operations.

- Functions vs App Service?
- How do you manage secrets?

- Blue/green vs canary?

- What do you monitor after release?
- What risks come with self-hosted agents?
- How do you discuss cloud cost as TPM?
