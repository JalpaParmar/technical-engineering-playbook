# System Design Interview Pack

## 45-minute structure
**5 min:** requirements/nonfunctional constraints.  
**5 min:** assumptions and rough scale.  
**10 min:** APIs/data/high-level architecture.  
**15 min:** deep dive into critical invariant/bottleneck.  
**5 min:** failure/security/observability.  
**5 min:** trade-offs/evolution.

## Avoid
Jumping to microservices, inventing huge scale without labeling assumptions, ignoring failures, saying “use cache” without invalidation, or treating a diagram as the answer.

Use the worked designs under [System Design](../05-System-Design/README.md).
