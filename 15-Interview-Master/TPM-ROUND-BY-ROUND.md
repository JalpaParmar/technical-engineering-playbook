# Technical Project Manager — Round-by-Round

## Round 1 — Recruiter / screening
Prepare: career story, role fit, technical foundation, team/stakeholder scope, availability, concise project example.

## Round 2 — PM delivery
Requirements, planning, Agile, dependencies, RAID, scope change, estimation, quality, releases, budget/resource awareness and stakeholder communication.

## Round 3 — Technical
Mobile/API architecture, system design fundamentals, cloud/DevOps, API readiness, debugging/incidents, security and engineering trade-offs.

## Round 4 — Leadership
Conflict, underperformance, executive escalation, critical incident, difficult trade-off, technical debt, mentoring and organizational alignment.

## Round 5 — AI / transformation
AI-assisted PM workflow, AI feature delivery, evaluation/guardrails, AI coding workflow, automation opportunities and responsible adoption.

## Final preparation
Choose 8–12 real stories that can answer multiple questions instead of memorizing 100 scripts.
