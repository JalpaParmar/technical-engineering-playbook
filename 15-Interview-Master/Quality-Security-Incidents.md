# Quality, Security & Incident Interview Pack

## Quality
Explain risk-based coverage, quality gates, contract/migration testing, flaky-test treatment and production signals.

## Security
Explain trust boundaries, authentication/authorization, least privilege, secrets and defensive supply-chain review.

## Incidents
Use: impact → stabilize → evidence → isolate → fix → validate → communicate → prevent.

## Strong scenario answer
Do not jump to a cause. State what is observed, what you would check next, safe mitigation options and how you would prove recovery.
