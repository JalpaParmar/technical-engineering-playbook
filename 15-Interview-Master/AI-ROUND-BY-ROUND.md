# AI Project Manager / AI-Enabled TPM — Round-by-Round

## AI foundations
LLMs, context, structured output, embeddings, RAG, agents/tools and evaluation.

## Delivery
Problem suitability, data/context readiness, model selection, guardrails, evaluation, rollout and monitoring.

## Risk
Hallucination, retrieval failure, prompt injection, data leakage, over-permissioned tools, cost/latency and human approval.

Requirements analysis, meeting/action extraction, risk summarization, reporting, incident evidence synthesis and engineering assistance.

## Leadership
Responsible adoption, measuring value, bias/error escalation, model/provider changes and organizational governance.
