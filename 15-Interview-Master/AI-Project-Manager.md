# AI Project Manager / AI-Enabled TPM Interview Pack

## Positioning
Lead AI-enabled delivery by connecting business outcome, data/context, engineering, evaluation, risk and operations. Do not present yourself as an ML researcher unless supported by experience.

## Framework
**Outcome → data/context → model/workflow → guardrails → evaluation → rollout → monitoring → iteration.**

## Questions
### How do you manage hallucination risk?
Ground where appropriate, constrain outputs, validate deterministic fields, evaluate representative cases, add human approval for high-impact use and monitor production.

### How do you measure AI feature success?
Business/task outcome plus quality/eval, safety, latency, cost and adoption—not model accuracy alone.

### Agent or workflow?
Prefer deterministic workflow when steps are known; use agentic behavior when dynamic planning/tool selection materially helps and can be bounded.

### How do you use AI day-to-day as TPM?
Summarization/action extraction, requirement structuring, risk analysis, status drafting, technical exploration and incident evidence synthesis—with human validation and data-policy compliance.
