# Mobile Tech Lead — Interview Pack

## Positioning
Demonstrate depth in mobile engineering and architecture, plus ability to lead cross-platform decisions, quality, incidents and delivery. Do not imply identical hands-on depth across platforms.

## High-value questions
### How do you choose mobile architecture?
Start with product/state/data complexity, team boundaries, lifetime, offline needs, testability and likely change. Choose the simplest structure that makes those changes safe.

### How do you manage iOS/Android consistency?
Align product behavior, API contracts, analytics, security and release criteria. Allow platform-native implementation differences when they improve correctness/UX.

### How do you handle a production mobile incident?
Bound impact/version/cohort, stabilize rollout/feature where possible, investigate with crash/API/release evidence, communicate known facts, validate recovery, then RCA/prevention.

### How do you lead modernization?
Identify measurable pain, protect behavior, choose incremental boundaries, avoid multi-dimensional rewrites, and measure delivery/quality improvement.

### How do you review a mobile design?
Review state ownership, data source of truth, API compatibility, offline/failure semantics, concurrency, security, testability, observability and rollout.
