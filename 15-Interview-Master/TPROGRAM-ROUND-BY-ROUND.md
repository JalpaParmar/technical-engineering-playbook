# Technical Program Manager — Round-by-Round

## Program focus
Multiple workstreams, cross-team architecture/dependencies, milestones, operating mechanisms, risk and executive decisions.

## Screening
Program scale, technical background, stakeholder complexity, delivery ownership.

## Program execution
Critical path, dependency governance, milestone confidence, scope/priority changes, program metrics and escalation.

## Technical
Architecture boundaries, API/platform dependencies, reliability, release coordination, cloud/DevOps and system evolution.

## Leadership
Conflicting organizational priorities, decision rights, executive communication, systemic delivery problems and engineering health.

## AI
Program-level automation, risk summarization, portfolio reporting, AI-enabled product governance and evaluation.
