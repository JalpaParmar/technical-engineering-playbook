# Estimation & Scope

Estimates express uncertainty; they are not promises of exact duration.

## Inputs
Known scope, complexity, dependencies, integration, test/release effort, unknowns, team capacity and historical evidence.

## Progressive refinement
Estimate broadly when ambiguity is high; refine as risks/contracts become clearer.

## Scope trade-off
When date/capacity is fixed, make scope priority explicit. Avoid silently trading away quality/security.

## Change
A new requirement should show impact on scope, schedule, cost/resources, risk and acceptance criteria before approval.
