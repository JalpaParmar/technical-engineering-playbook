# Ambiguity → Problem Definition

Do not convert a vague request directly into tickets.

## Clarify
Who has the problem? What outcome is needed? Current behavior? Desired behavior? Constraints? Evidence? Deadline driver? What is explicitly out of scope?

## Problem statement
**For [user], [current problem] causes [impact]. We need [outcome], subject to [constraints].**

Avoid embedding the preferred solution unless already decided.

## Questions
- What business rule is authoritative?
- What happens today?
- Which platforms/roles/locations?
- What edge/failure cases matter?
- What does success look like?
- What remains unknown?

## TPM value
Visible unknowns are safer than false certainty. Maintain an assumptions/open-questions list until resolved.
