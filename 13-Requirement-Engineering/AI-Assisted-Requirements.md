# AI-Assisted Requirement Engineering

AI can summarize discovery notes, identify ambiguities, propose story decomposition, draft acceptance criteria, find conflicting wording and generate test ideas.

## Safe workflow
Source material → AI draft → human/domain review → technical review → approved requirement.

## Risks
AI can invent business rules, silently resolve ambiguity or produce plausible but unsupported acceptance criteria.

## Prompt pattern
“Extract only stated requirements. Separate assumptions and open questions. Do not invent missing rules. Then propose candidate acceptance criteria labeled as proposals.”

Human stakeholders remain authoritative for product/business decisions.
