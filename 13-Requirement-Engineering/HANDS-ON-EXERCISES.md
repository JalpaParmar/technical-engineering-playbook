# Requirement Engineering Exercises

1. Turn “improve booking cancellation” into problem statement, open questions, stories and acceptance criteria.
2. Add NFRs to a payment feature without inventing numbers.
3. Build dependency plan when three APIs are late.
4. Analyze a mid-sprint change request and present options.
5. Give AI messy meeting notes; identify which generated rules are unsupported.
6. Create traceability from business outcome to release validation.
