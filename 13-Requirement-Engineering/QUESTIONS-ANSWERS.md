# Requirement Engineering Q&A

**Vague requirement?** Clarify user/problem/outcome, current/desired behavior, constraints, edge cases and success; track assumptions/open questions.

**Acceptance criteria?** Observable testable product behavior, including important error/permission/edge states.

**API late?** Lock contract/examples, parallelize with mocks where safe, track integration risk and validate against real API before completion.

**Scope creep?** Identify change, quantify impact/options, get decision, update baseline/acceptance and communicate.

**AI for requirements?** Great for extraction/decomposition and gap detection; never let it invent unapproved business rules.
