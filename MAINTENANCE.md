# Maintenance Guide

## Monthly / before important interviews
- review target-role technologies for version-sensitive changes;
- refresh relevant 30-minute sheet and role pack;
- add only high-value missing questions/scenarios.

## Quarterly
- inspect broken/renamed internal links after structural changes;
- review official platform/cloud/AI documentation for pages marked version-sensitive;
- archive/supersede obsolete guidance rather than silently rewriting historical decisions.

## After a real project/incident
Add generalized learning only when confidentiality permits. Never copy proprietary/customer-sensitive information.

## Content rule
Prefer updating a canonical page and linking to it over creating another explanation.

## Experience rule
Generic playbook content and personal interview stories are different assets. Personal stories must be factual and should not acquire invented metrics through later edits.

## AI-assisted maintenance
AI may propose updates, detect duplicates and check navigation. Human review remains required for technical accuracy and experience claims.
