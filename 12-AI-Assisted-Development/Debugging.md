# AI-Assisted Debugging

Provide sanitized evidence: symptom, scope, timeline, logs/stack, relevant code/config and recent changes.

Ask AI to separate:
- observed facts;
- missing evidence;
- ranked hypotheses;
- tests that would confirm/falsify each hypothesis.

Do not ask it to “find the root cause” from insufficient evidence and then treat the answer as fact.

Production mitigations remain human-approved.
