# Codex, Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude & Cursor

Capabilities evolve quickly; verify current product documentation before relying on feature-specific claims.

## Selection dimensions
Repository/terminal access, inline completion, multi-file changes, planning/reasoning, context management, enterprise/privacy controls and team workflow.

## Practical pattern
- inline assistant: small local edits/completions;
- repository agent: multi-file implementation/refactor/test work;
- conversational reasoning: architecture, debugging hypotheses, requirements and review;
- human engineer: owns decisions and merge/release.

Do not choose tools from brand popularity alone.
