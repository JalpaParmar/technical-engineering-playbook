# AI-Assisted Development Q&A

**How do you use AI without lowering quality?** Constrain scope, require evidence/diffs, run deterministic checks and keep human ownership of architecture/security/release.

**AI-generated tests?** Useful for coverage ideas/scaffolding, but validate assertions and independence from implementation.

**AI for legacy migration?** Use it to map and propose incremental seams; protect behavior first and avoid unreviewed bulk rewrites.

**AI debugging?** Great for evidence summarization and hypothesis generation; root cause needs validation.

**Which AI coding tool?** Choose from workflow/context/repository access/privacy and measured quality, not brand alone.
