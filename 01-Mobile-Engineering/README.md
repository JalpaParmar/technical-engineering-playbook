## Depth intent
iOS and mobile architecture receive the deepest treatment because they form the primary mobile engineering foundation. Android is built to strong working/architecture depth without inventing hands-on experience.

## How to study
**Learn → explain → apply → troubleshoot.** Read a concept, answer its Q&A aloud, work through the scenario/checklist, then explain the trade-off without looking at the notes.

## Phase 2 quality bar
Content must include reasoning and trade-offs, not just definitions. Examples are educational unless explicitly supported as real experience. Platform/version-sensitive details should be verified against current official documentation before production use.
