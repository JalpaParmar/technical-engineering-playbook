# Threat Modeling Fundamentals

Defensive threat modeling asks what assets matter, who/what crosses trust boundaries, how misuse could occur and which controls reduce risk.

## Lightweight process
1. map data/components/trust boundaries;
2. identify sensitive assets/actions;
3. enumerate realistic threats/misuse;
4. assess impact/likelihood with organizational method;
5. choose controls;
6. validate and revisit after architecture change.

Use established frameworks such as STRIDE when useful, but the framework is not the outcome.
