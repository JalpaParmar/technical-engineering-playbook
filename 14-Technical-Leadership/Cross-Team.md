# Dependencies & Cross-Team Leadership

Align on contracts, owners, milestones, test environments and escalation triggers.

## API/mobile example
Backend and mobile can work in parallel from an agreed contract/mock, but integration remains a milestone.

## Conflict
When teams disagree, restate shared outcome and constraints, gather evidence, identify decision owner and document the decision/trade-off.

## Escalation
Escalation is a delivery mechanism, not blame. Bring impact, options and required decision.
