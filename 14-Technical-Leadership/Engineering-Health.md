# Technical Debt & Engineering Health

Engineering health includes build/test reliability, deployment feedback, dependency age/risk, incident patterns, observability, maintainability and developer toil.

## Metrics
Use trends to identify system problems, not rank individuals.

## Debt
Prioritize debt tied to incidents, security, blocked roadmap, excessive lead time or recurring toil.

## Capacity
Reserve explicit capacity or attach debt treatment to roadmap work where it reduces future risk. Avoid invisible “cleanup” commitments.
