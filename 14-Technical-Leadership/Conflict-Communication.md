# Conflict & Stakeholder Communication

## Technical disagreement
Clarify decision, constraints and evidence. Separate factual disagreement from preference. Run a small experiment/prototype when it can resolve uncertainty cheaply.

## Stakeholders
Translate technical detail into impact, options, risk and decision needed.

Bad: “Architecture is bad.”  
Better: “Current synchronous dependency adds a single failure path to checkout; options are X/Y with these delivery and reliability trade-offs.”

## Difficult news
Communicate early with current facts, confidence level, mitigation and next checkpoint.
