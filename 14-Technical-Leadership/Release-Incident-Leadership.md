# Release & Incident Leadership

## Release
Confirm scope, integration, quality/security, migrations, dependencies, flags, observability, rollout and mitigation.

## Incident
Protect users/data → establish owner → stabilize → investigate → communicate → validate → learn.

Technical leaders prevent hypothesis from becoming “fact” under pressure and make decision ownership explicit.

## After
Ensure corrective actions improve systems/tests/monitoring/process rather than producing a document only.
