# Technical Leadership Q&A

**Engineer disagrees with architecture?** Clarify constraints/evidence, compare options/trade-offs, prototype if useful, use accountable decision owner and document consequential choice.

**Developer underperforming?** First clarify expectations/evidence and blockers; give specific feedback/support, agree measurable follow-up and use organizational people processes appropriately.

**Deadline at risk?** Quantify remaining critical path, dependencies and confidence; present scope/resource/date options without silently cutting quality.

**Tech debt vs features?** Translate debt into business/delivery/incident risk and prioritize transparently.

**Production incident?** Stabilize safely, investigate with evidence, communicate facts and validate recovery before RCA/prevention.
