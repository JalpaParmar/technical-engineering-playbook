# Architecture Decision Playbook

## Trigger
A consequential technical choice with meaningful long-term trade-offs.

## Process
1. define decision/problem, not preferred technology;
2. capture constraints and assumptions;
3. identify realistic options including “do nothing/current”;
4. compare changeability, reliability, security, delivery, operations and cost;
5. prototype/measure uncertain high-risk assumptions where useful;
6. decide with accountable owner;
7. record ADR;
8. define validation/revisit trigger.

## Avoid
Architecture by fashion, false precision, hidden assumptions and decisions that never get revisited after constraints change.
