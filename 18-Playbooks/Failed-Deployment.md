# Failed Deployment Playbook

## Detect
Confirm user/service impact and distinguish deployment failure from application degradation.

## Stabilize
Stop rollout; rollback/switch traffic/disable feature where safe; protect data/downstream systems.

## Investigate
Deployment logs, artifact/version, config/secrets, migrations, dependency health, infrastructure changes and telemetry.

## Communicate
Known impact, mitigation, evidence and next checkpoint—do not call a hypothesis root cause.

## Close
Validate recovery from production signals, then capture root cause/contributing factors and improve pipeline/runbook/architecture.
