# Production Incident Playbook

## Trigger
Material production degradation, outage, data risk or critical workflow failure.

## Execute
Assess impact → establish owner → stabilize safely → gather evidence → investigate in parallel → communicate known facts → validate recovery → RCA/prevention.

## Communication
State impact, known evidence, mitigation and next checkpoint. Never label an unverified hypothesis as root cause.

## Closure
Recovery is confirmed by user/service signals, not only a developer test. Significant incidents produce actionable follow-up with owners.
