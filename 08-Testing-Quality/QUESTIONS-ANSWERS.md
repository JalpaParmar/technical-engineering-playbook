# Testing & Quality Q&A

**Unit vs integration?** Unit isolates behavior for fast feedback; integration validates real component collaboration.

**Why not automate everything?** Automation has build/maintenance cost; prioritize repeatable high-risk behavior and keep exploratory/human judgment where it adds value.

**Flaky test?** Treat as a product-delivery defect: classify cause, fix determinism/ownership, quarantine only temporarily with accountability.

**Coverage target?** Coverage is a diagnostic, not a quality score. Risk and meaningful assertions matter more than a universal percentage.

**Quality ownership?** Whole team. QA specialization adds expertise but does not transfer engineering/product responsibility.
