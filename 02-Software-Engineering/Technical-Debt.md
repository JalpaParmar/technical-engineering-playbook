# Technical Debt

Technical debt describes choices/conditions that increase future change or operational cost. Not every imperfection deserves remediation.

Classify: deliberate, accidental, architecture constraint, obsolete dependency, test/observability gap, operational debt.

Prioritize using delivery lead time, defects/incidents, security/support risk, toil and blocked roadmap.

**Product discussion:** translate debt into business impact/options—what becomes slower/riskier, cost of deferral, and incremental treatment—not “clean code” preference.
