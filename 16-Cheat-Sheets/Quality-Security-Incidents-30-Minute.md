# Quality + Security + Incidents — 30-Minute Revision

**0–5:** risk-based testing; unit/integration/contract/UI.  
**5–10:** quality gates, flaky tests, quality metrics.  
**10–15:** threat model, auth/authz, least privilege, secrets.  
**15–20:** supply chain, API/mobile defensive security.  
**20–25:** evidence-driven debugging, logs/metrics/traces.  
**25–30:** incident command, mitigation, RCA and prevention.
