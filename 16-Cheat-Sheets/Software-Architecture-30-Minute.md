# Software Architecture — 30-Minute Revision

**0–5:** SOLID, composition, coupling/cohesion, abstraction trade-offs.  
**5–10:** monolith/modular monolith/microservices.  
**10–15:** distributed failure, timeout/retry/idempotency/consistency.  
**15–20:** queues/events, duplicate/order/schema evolution.  
**20–25:** caching/scaling/resilience/observability.  
**25–30:** ADRs + one system-design walkthrough.

Speak aloud: Why not microservices? How do retries cause outages? How do you prevent duplicate payment? What does eventual consistency mean to a user? What signals prove architecture is healthy?
