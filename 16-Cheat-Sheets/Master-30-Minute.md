# Technical Engineering Playbook — 30-Minute Master Revision

## 0–5: Mobile
State/lifecycle, concurrency, API/offline, architecture, release/observability.

## 5–10: Architecture
SOLID/abstraction, monolith vs services, consistency, retries/idempotency, events/resilience.

## 10–15: Backend/Cloud/DevOps
API contracts/auth/errors, SQL/cache/queues, Azure identity/services, CI/CD, rollout.

## 15–20: Quality/Security/Incidents
Risk-based tests, quality gates, threat boundaries, least privilege, evidence-driven debugging/RCA.

## 20–25: AI
RAG, embeddings, agents/tools, evals/grounding, security/privacy, human validation.

## 25–30: TPM/Leadership
Requirements/NFRs, dependencies, scope, technical decisions, debt, stakeholder communication.

### Final five questions
1. What is the critical invariant?
2. What happens when a dependency fails?
3. What evidence supports the decision?
4. How will we validate production?
5. What trade-off am I accepting?
