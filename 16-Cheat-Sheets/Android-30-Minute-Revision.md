# Android — 30-Minute Revision

## 0–5 min
Kotlin null safety/data/sealed classes; lifecycle; ViewModel.

## 5–10 min
Compose state, recomposition, effects, navigation and accessibility.

## 10–15 min
Coroutines, scopes, cancellation, Flow and lifecycle-aware collection.

## 15–20 min
Retrofit/OkHttp, Room/migrations, repository/source of truth, WorkManager.

## 20–25 min
Hilt/Dagger concepts, Firebase capabilities, testing, ANR/performance.

## 25–30 min
API compatibility, feature flags, Play release, staged rollout, observability.

## Speak these aloud
1. Why can Compose trigger duplicate work?
2. How would I investigate an ANR?
3. What belongs in a ViewModel?
4. How would I test Room migration?
5. How do I discuss Android credibly without overstating hands-on experience?
