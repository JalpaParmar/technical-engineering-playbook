# Interview-Day Quick Sheet

## Before
Review target JD, 5 role requirements, 5 relevant real stories and the role pack. Do not attempt to memorize the whole repository.

## During
Answer the question first. Add context only as needed. If unclear, clarify. If you do not know, explain what you know and how you would investigate.

## Technical
Definition/position → trade-off → example → validation.

## PM
Impact → options → decision → action/result.

## Incident
Impact → stabilize → evidence → communicate → validate.

## AI
Outcome → architecture/workflow → evaluation → guardrails → monitoring.

## Avoid
Invented metrics, pretending hands-on depth you do not have, giant 5-minute answers, buzzword chains and claiming AI output is automatically correct.
